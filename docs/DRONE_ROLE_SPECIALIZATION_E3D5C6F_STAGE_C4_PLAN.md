## Proposed implementation
- Default-off exact specialized C4 route; preserve ordinary B2/B3 and C1-C3 fixtures. Reuse C3 anchored physical delivery/escrow receipt and services; explicit B2→B3 admission only after empty logistics docked, exact receipt, powered allocation, zero builder cargo, pinned original job and custody ticket. Register pole into existing network and configure scheduler/tracking without replacing coordinator/escrow/charging. Capture original anchored custody ticket before builder admission, including exact escrow ID; retain for cancellation. No automatic dispatcher or parallel site timer.
- Extend finite charging with guarded right builder berth and distinct token; use same allocator/spare-generation budget. Do not mutate batteries directly. Logistics remains docked throughout active building and blackout. Explicit owner transfer only after preflight; reject foreign receipt, duplicate owner, wrong role and stale assignments.
- Blackout while outbound: stop unsafe work, meter return, keep original QUEUED/PAUSED job and escrow, release allocation/readiness. Blackout while working: coordinator reconciles real loss of power, freezes exact progress and removes active slot; initiate metered empty return, keep assignment identity and receipt (not terminal). No refund on power loss. Dock and wait if unpowered; no free recharge. Restore power, recharge/forecast full safe trip; resume same job only after actual arrival, original escrow and receipt, real power and scheduler readiness. Repeated interruption during resume safely parks again. Preserve original binding when safe or add exact guarded PAUSED rebind via scheduler; no fake QUEUED reset. No progress while traveling or parked. Normal completion consumes escrow exactly once and produces real building; no recovery ticket activation for completed job.
- Cancellation while outbound, working, parking, docked or resuming: original job terminal, revoke receipt and builder claim; scheduler/coordinator remove exact tracking/readiness; original escrow refunds only into locked staging, blocked settlement stays protected. Builder returns empty to right berth or remains docked, no further progress. No new worker/receipt or cargo authority. Record exact cancellation and terminal builder cleanup; builder must be physically home, unbound and inert before ownership passes back B3→B2, never two owners. Exact terminal authorization for builder-bearing revoked lease, without weakening C3 checks for unclaimed leases. Reuse C3 recovery transport and custody ticket; C3 logistics empty delivery assignment must be cleaned/recharged before staged mission. Only logistics collects/deposits five ingots; builder remains inert. If refund blocked or destination unavailable, wait safely. No invented stock, remote refunds, teleport, free charging or unlock of original staging.
- Stage C4 tests isolated in existing opt-in scene via new fixture; Main, accepted scenes/tests, local changes and unmerged draft PR #1 preserved. C5 fresh full regression and manual verification separately. No fleet scheduling, source destruction, generalized automatic recovery or Main activation.
